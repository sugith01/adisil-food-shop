Our story

Where tradition meets taste

ADISIL began as a single stall serving dosas to a hungry morning crowd. What stayed constant as we grew was the way we cook: unhurried, ingredient-first, and rooted in recipes passed down through generations of South Indian kitchens.

Today every dish still starts from scratch — spices ground in-house, batters fermented overnight, and gravies simmered slowly rather than rushed. It's traditional cooking, served for how people eat today.
